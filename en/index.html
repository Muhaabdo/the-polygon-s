FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is VIBE Real Estate the developer?

No. VIBE is an independent broker that presents and markets projects from different developers, including Palm Hills Developments. Contracts are signed directly with the developer.

What is the lowest down payment in the Palm Hills projects shown?

The down payment in the five projects shown is 5% of the unit price.

What is the longest installment period?

Up to 10 years in PX and Bamboo III, and up to 8 years in Palm Parks, Crown Extension and Palm Hills One.

When is delivery?

From 2028 in PX, 2029 in Palm Parks and Bamboo III, and 2030 in Crown Extension and Palm Hills One.

Are the prices shown final?

Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer without notice. The developer is the final reference at the time of contracting.

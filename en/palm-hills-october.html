About Palm Hills October

Palm Hills October is an integrated residential community in West Cairo by Palm Hills Developments. The company keeps launching new projects and phases around it, including the ones shown on this page.

On West Cairo's main roads

Close to the 26th of July Axis, the Cairo–Alexandria Desert Road and the Dahshur Link.

FAQ

Palm Hills October: frequently asked questions

Which Palm Hills October projects are available?

PX Compound (apartments, town houses, villas); Palm Parks (apartments); Crown Extension (family homes, twin houses); Palm Hills One (town houses, twin houses, villas); Bamboo III (family homes, town houses). All are developed by Palm Hills Developments.

What are the starting prices in Palm Hills October?

Prices start from EGP 13,144,000 in PX Compound. By project: PX Compound from EGP 13,144,000; Palm Parks from EGP 17,807,000; Crown Extension from EGP 30,521,000; Palm Hills One from EGP 47,549,000; Bamboo III from EGP 27,457,000.

What payment plans are available?

The down payment is 5% in every project shown. Installments: PX Compound up to 10 years; Palm Parks up to 8 years; Crown Extension up to 8 years; Palm Hills One up to 8 years; Bamboo III up to 10 years.

When is delivery?

PX Compound 2028; Palm Parks 2029; Crown Extension 2030; Palm Hills One 2030; Bamboo III 2029, according to the developer.

Where can I find apartments, and where villas?

Apartments are in PX Compound, Palm Parks. Family homes and town houses are in Bamboo III, PX Compound, Crown Extension, Palm Hills One. Twin houses and villas are in PX Compound, Crown Extension, Palm Hills One.

Is VIBE Real Estate the developer?

No. The developer is Palm Hills Developments. VIBE Real Estate is an independent broker that presents the projects and helps you compare and choose; contracts are signed directly with the developer.

Are the prices shown final?

Prices are indicative and may be changed by the developer without notice. The developer is the final reference at the time of contracting.

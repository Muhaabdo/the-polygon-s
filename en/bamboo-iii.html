About Bamboo III

Everything you need to know in a minute

Bamboo III is the third phase of Bamboo by Palm Hills Developments, inside Palm Hills October near Gezira Club and overlooking the 26th of July Axis.

The project covers around 56 acres with lagoons, water features and wide green areas. Homes are family homes with a garden or penthouse plus middle and corner town houses — all fully finished.

About Palm Parks

Everything you need to know in a minute

Palm Parks is a Palm Hills Developments community in West Cairo, right off the Dahshur Link, designed as a quiet low-density neighbourhood with open space between buildings.

The project is all apartments with practical layouts, with a clubhouse, swimming pools and a commercial area inside. Only a limited number of units is currently available.

About us

Your compass in Egypt's property market

VIBE Real Estate is an independent broker — we are not the developer of any project shown on this website.

VIBE Real Estate is an independent property marketing platform, specialised in presenting the best residential and investment opportunities from Egypt's leading developers — from New Cairo and West Cairo to the New Capital and the North Coast.

We believe buying a property is one of the most important decisions in anyone's life, whether it is a forever home or an investment. So we simplify the search: we bring together trusted projects, show prices and payment plans with full transparency, and help you compare and choose with confidence — with no pressure.

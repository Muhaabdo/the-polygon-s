About the project

About PX Compound

Everything you need to know in a minute

PX is Palm Hills Developments' newest community in West Cairo, set on the highest point of Palm Hills October — which gives its homes open views across the whole area.

The project spans around 370 feddans with a master plan by Chapman Taylor, split into residential zones of apartments and town houses, with a clubhouse, a commercial hub and walking trails inside the compound.

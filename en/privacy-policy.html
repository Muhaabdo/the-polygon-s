Privacy Policy

Your privacy is a priority — here is exactly how we handle your data

Welcome to VIBE Real Estate. We are an independent property marketing platform presenting residential and investment opportunities from different developers in Egypt. This policy explains what data we collect when you use our website, how we use and protect it, and your rights. By using the website you agree to the practices described here.

Data we collect

Data you give us directly:

  • Your name and phone number when you fill in any enquiry form on the website
  • The project and unit type you choose in the form
  • The content of your messages when you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone
  • Your property preferences (unit type, area, budget) if you share them with us

Data collected automatically:

  • IP address, device type, browser and operating system
  • Pages you visit, time on site and the source of your visit (such as Google Ads and the ad click identifier)
  • Cookie data and similar tracking technologies

How we use your data

  • To contact you about the projects and units you showed interest in
  • To answer your enquiries and send prices, payment plans and available offers
  • To improve the browsing experience and website content based on usage
  • To measure and improve our advertising campaigns
  • To comply with any legal or regulatory requirements

Sharing your data

Because we work as a property marketing broker, we may share your data (name, phone number and property preferences) with the developer or the authorised sales team responsible for the project you showed interest in, solely to follow up your request and provide details.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, and we do not share it with anyone not directly involved in your request.

We may disclose data only if required by law or by an order from a competent authority.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: needed for the website to work and cannot be disabled
  • Analytics cookies: such as Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use the website
  • Advertising cookies: such as Google Ads, to measure ad effectiveness and show personalised ads (remarketing) based on your visit

You can control or delete cookies from your browser settings at any time. You can also opt out of Google personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.

Data protection

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse, including HTTPS encryption and restricting access to authorised people only. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or to comply with legal obligations. When it is no longer needed it is securely deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

At any time you may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data from our records
  • Object to the use of your data for marketing and ask us to stop contacting you
  • Withdraw your consent to the processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as possible.

External links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as WhatsApp or official developer websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults only (18 and over), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we learn that data from a minor has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

Contact us about privacy

For any question or request about your personal data or this policy, email us at:
